If you've visited the site lately, you might have noticed things look a little different. We're rebuilding The Common's website from the ground up — faster, warmer, and built so the calendar is always full of what's actually on.

The goal is simple: make it easy to see the space, find your people, and book the way that fits you. Drop in for a day, take a desk part-time, or grab a private office — and either way, the community comes with it.

We're also working on something we're excited about: **The Common Card**, a membership pass that lives in your phone wallet and unlocks perks across town — plus discounted access at partner spaces up and down the Sea-to-Sky corridor. More on that this summer.

Want to be first to know? Join the newsletter, or just come to a Thursday social and say hello.
