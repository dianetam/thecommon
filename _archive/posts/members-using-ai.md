Our members skew builder — founders, consultants, remote pros — so AI comes up a lot at the Thursday social. Here are five genuinely useful ways people here are using it (no hype, all practical).

### 1. Drafting the boring stuff
Proposals, follow-up emails, scope docs. Members start with an AI draft and edit — cutting the blank-page tax.

### 2. Building a website in an evening
Our most-requested workshop. With today's tools you can go from idea to a live site in a single evening — no code. (We run this as part of the AI Series.)

### 3. Cleaning up data and admin
Reformatting spreadsheets, summarizing call notes, turning a messy export into something usable.

### 4. Research that used to take a day
Market scans, competitor snapshots, "what am I missing" checks before a big decision.

### 5. Learning out loud
Members use AI as a patient tutor for new skills — then bring questions to the community to pressure-test.
